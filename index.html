How To Use The App
START HERE

Welcome to My Progress

This app tracks your progress, streak, XP, levels, workouts, quests and goals. Your information is saved on your device.

🔥 Streak

Your streak shows how many full days have passed since your Recovery Start date. Change the date from Profile whenever you need to.

⭐ XP & Levels

Complete quests and workouts to earn XP. As your XP increases, your level increases. The bar on Home shows your progress toward the next level.

✅ Daily Quests

Open Quests every day and complete the tasks. Each completed quest gives XP. Quests reset for the next day.

🏋️ Workouts

Open Workout, enter your workout information and press Complete Workout. You'll receive 100 XP and the day will appear on your calendar.

🌳 Growth Tree
🌱 → 🌿 → 🌳 → 🌲

Your tree changes as your streak grows. It's a visual representation of your consistency.

👤 Profile

Profile lets you set your main goal, change your start date, write in your journal and reset your streak.

🛡️ I'm Struggling

Use this button when you're having a difficult moment. It gives you a reminder to pause and think before acting on a temporary urge.
